How creation works

A course is generated from subject matter: the brief goes to OpenMAIC, the classroom comes back, and the syllabus, quizzes and final examination come from the same text. Certificates and paid enrolment are configured at creation, with the same rules everywhere.

Programs compose courses into semesters; classrooms are the individual scenes inside a course. Nothing here duplicates another page: each action lives where the thing it changes is listed.
