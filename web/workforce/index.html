Focused AI Skills Training (FAST AI)

Instructor-led, applied AI training for the tools and tasks a worker already does. Six stackable courses take an employee from how a model behaves to prompting, analysis, occupation-specific workflows, management and governance — each course a set of live multi-agent classrooms with exercises built from the employer's own work.

Credential: FAST AI — Applied AI Skills · Audience: Massachusetts employers and the incumbent or newly hired workers they enroll

AI literacyprompt engineeringAI productivityAI-assisted analysisworkflow automationAI for managersAI governance and risk

What it costs an employer

No cost to an eligible employer. Commonwealth Corporation purchases training slots from approved providers and reimburses completed enrollments, so the employer pays no tuition for approved courses.

Administered by Commonwealth Corporation, a quasi-public arm of the Massachusetts Executive Office of Labor and Workforce Development, with the Executive Office of Economic Development, funded by the Workforce Investment Trust Fund.

Program terms

  • Course costs may not exceed $300 per instructional hour per training slot, or $3,000 per person per course, whichever is less.
  • A provider's total award may not exceed $400,000, covering all of its approved courses.
  • Contracts run for up to two years; match contributions are not required.
  • Enrollment, attendance and completion are tracked and reported to Commonwealth Corporation by the provider.

How the training runs

  • Instructor-led: every chapter runs as a live multi-agent classroom — an AI teacher, AI classmates, and the learner doing the work.
  • Applied: each exercise uses an artifact from the worker's own role, tools and day-to-day tasks rather than generic AI literacy content.
  • Tracked: attendance, completion and assessment are recorded in the LMS and reported to the program.
  • Stackable: the six courses can be taken as a certificate ladder or individually against an employer's training plan.

Timeline

  • Provider RFQ applications were due November 6, 2026, with award decisions expected in mid-December 2026.
  • The program opens for employer enrollment in January 2027.
  • Training is prioritised where it starts on or after February 1, 2027 and finishes by June 30, 2028.

Taken as a ladder they form the FAST AI — Applied AI Skills certificate; taken individually, each stands on its own against an employer's training plan.

What a worker can do afterwards

  • Explain what a language model does, where it fails, and how to verify an AI draft before it is used.
  • Write prompts and build repeatable AI workflows for the work the learner already owns.
  • Analyse data and produce reports with AI assistance, checking the result against the source.
  • Apply AI to the tasks of a specific occupation, with that occupation's own examples and constraints.
  • Lead AI adoption: choose use cases, set review steps, and measure whether the work improved.
  • Govern AI use: data handling, disclosure, bias and risk review under an organisation's policy.
