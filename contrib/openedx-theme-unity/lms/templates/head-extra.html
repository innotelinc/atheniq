## mako ## AthenIQ — Unity, in the head, before the first paint. ## ## `main.html` includes `head-extra.html` on every server-rendered LMS page, and ## it is the only hook in the `` a theme gets. That makes it the right ## place for three things: ## ## 1. **The palette.** Unity's tokens (`unity-theme.css`) and the LMS skin ## (`atheniq-lms.css`) load here rather than at the end of the body, so a ## page never paints the base theme's colours and then repaints ours. ## ## 2. **The scheme.** `desk` — the training range's register, which is what a ## learning platform is — is set on `` before anything renders. ## ## 3. **The mode.** Unity's switch is the header control; this adopts the ## legacy `selected-paragon-theme-variant` cookie (written by the theme the ## deployment used before Unity) into Unity's storage once, so a browser ## that already picked dark does not have to pick again. Unity's own ## `data-mode` is the source of truth from then on. ## ## `UnityTheme` owns the tokens; the listener at the bottom keeps the compiled ## base stylesheet's dark-mode class in step with `data-mode`.
