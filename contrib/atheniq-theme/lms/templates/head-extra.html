## mako ## AthenIQ — Unity, in the head, before the first paint. ## ## `main.html` includes `head-extra.html` on every server-rendered LMS page, and ## it is the only hook in the `` a theme gets. That makes it the right ## place for three things: ## ## 1. **The palette.** Unity's tokens (`unity-theme.css`) and the LMS skin ## (`atheniq-lms.css`) load here rather than at the end of the body, so a ## page never paints Indigo's colours and then repaints ours. ## ## 2. **The scheme.** `desk` — the training range's register, which is what a ## learning platform is — is set on `` before anything renders. ## ## 3. **The mode.** Unity and Indigo each have a light/dark switch. They are ## the same decision, so this adopts Indigo's cookie (`selected-paragon- ## theme-variant`, which its toggle already writes) into Unity's storage ## and lets Unity paint from it. Without that adoption the two would ## disagree: Indigo's `body.indigo-dark-theme` and Unity's `data-mode` ## would flip at different times, and half the page would lag the other. ## ## The switch itself is Indigo's existing header control — no new widget, and no ## second place to change the appearance. `UnityTheme` owns the tokens; the ## listener at the bottom keeps Indigo's compiled SCSS class in step.
