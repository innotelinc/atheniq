ITSP102 — Networking & Systems Support

The second course of the Innotel Labs IT Support Specialist certification track. Go beyond troubleshooting basics into the systems that run a network: architecture and subnets, routing and wireless, Windows Server and Active Directory, and Linux administration.

What you will learn

  • Network architecture, subnetting, and the layered model
  • Switching, VLANs, routing, NAT, and wireless
  • Windows Server, AD DS, DNS, and Group Policy
  • Linux services, packages, permissions, and hardening
