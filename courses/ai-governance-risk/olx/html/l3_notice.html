Notice, Explanation, Monitoring

People are entitled to know when a decision about them is assisted, what the system does, and how to challenge it. The practical questions are: who must be told, what must the notice say, and where does monitoring stop being management and start being surveillance?

What a useful notice says

  • What is assisted. The specific task, not "AI is used".
  • What the system does not decide. The human decision and its owner.
  • What data is used, and where it came from.
  • How to ask a question or challenge a decision, with a route that works.
  • When it was last reviewed. A date signals maintenance.

"This process may use automated tools" fails all five. It informs nobody and protects nobody.

Explaining a decision

An explanation for the affected person is not a feature-importance chart. It is a short account of the factors that mattered, in plain language, and what would have changed the outcome. A model's internal weights are not an explanation a person can use; the decision process around the model is.

Monitoring boundaries

Productivity monitoring is a different question from AI assistance. Before deploying anything that observes people — keystroke logging, screen capture, sentiment analysis of messages — ask:

  1. What decision will this data inform?
  2. Have the people affected been told, and did they have a voice?
  3. Is the measure a proxy that punishes legitimate work (breaks, care work, complex cases)?
  4. How long is it retained, and who can see it?

If the answers are unclear, the deployment is not ready — regardless of what the vendor's dashboards show.

Jurisdiction

Disclosure, consultation and monitoring duties vary by country, sector, and in some places by works council agreement. This chapter teaches the practice; confirm the legal requirements for your deployment with counsel before you rely on them.

Try this

Write the notice for one assisted process in your organisation, then read it as the person affected. Can they tell what happens, who decides, and how to object? If not, rewrite it until they can.
