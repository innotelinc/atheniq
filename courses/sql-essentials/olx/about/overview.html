SQL101 — SQL Essentials

The second course of the Innotel Labs Data Foundations track. It adds the query language to the data literacy of DATA101: reading a schema, filtering and aggregating, joining tables, and changing data safely.

What you will learn

  • The relational model: tables, keys, relationships, and why normalisation
  • SELECT with filtering, sorting, expressions and aliases
  • Aggregating with GROUP BY / HAVING and joining tables
  • Defining and changing data safely with DDL, DML, transactions and constraints
