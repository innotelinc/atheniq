MAIC101 — AI Classroom Facilitation

The core course of the Innotel Labs AI Classroom Facilitator track — for instructors and trainers who will run generative-AI classrooms. It covers using generative AI well, authoring a classroom with OpenMAIC, facilitating live sessions, and assessing learners ethically.

What you will learn

  • What generative AI is good at, and where it must be verified or refused
  • Authoring an OpenMAIC classroom: scenario, agents, source material, activity
  • Facilitating live sessions and recovering when the AI fails
  • Assessment design, disclosure, bias and equity in an AI-present classroom
