DATA101 — Data Foundations

The first course of the Innotel Labs Data Foundations track. It takes an operations or support professional from reading a spreadsheet to producing a trustworthy report — the data literacy every operations role now expects, before any SQL.

What you will learn

  • The vocabulary of data and what "tidy" means in practice
  • A repeatable cleaning pass: profile, standardise, de-duplicate, validate
  • Choosing a chart from the question, and reporting without misleading
  • Sources of truth, governance, privacy, and data quality as an ongoing habit
