ITSP103 — Security Operations Foundations

The third course of the Innotel Labs IT Support Specialist certification track. Move from the security fundamentals of ITSP101 into the everyday work of a security operations team: monitoring, identity administration, layered defense, and incident response.

What you will learn

  • Monitoring, alert triage, and SOC metrics
  • Identity and access administration: RBAC, MFA, joiner-mover-leaver, PAM
  • Endpoint and network defense in depth
  • The incident-response lifecycle and evidence handling
