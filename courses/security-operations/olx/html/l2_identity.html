Identity & Access Administration

Identity is the control plane of security. Almost every breach involves a misuse of identity — a stolen password, an over-privileged account, an account that should have been closed.

Core principles

  • Least privilege — the minimum access for the job, for the shortest time.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) — grant access to roles, not individuals.
  • Separation of duties — no single person can both make and approve a sensitive change.
  • MFA everywhere — especially for administrators.

Joiner, mover, leaver

EventAction
JoinerCreate one identity; grant the role's baseline access
MoverRemove old access before adding new access
LeaverDisable immediately, then remove access and recover assets

Privileged access

Administrator accounts are the crown jewels. Use separate admin accounts, just-in-time elevation, and a privileged access management (PAM) tool that records sessions. Support never shares a privileged login, even for convenience.

Access reviews

On a schedule, every owner confirms who still needs access. Reviews find the accounts that rotation, transfers, and forgotten projects leave behind — the ones attackers like best.
