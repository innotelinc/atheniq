Security Operations & Monitoring

A security operations centre (SOC) watches for the signs of an attack and responds. Support technicians feed it the raw signals — alerts, tickets, odd behaviour — and act on its decisions.

What the SOC does

  • Collect — logs and events from endpoints, servers, network, identity, and cloud.
  • Detect — correlate them against rules and known attacker behaviour (a SIEM or EDR platform).
  • Triage — decide which alerts are real, and how urgent.
  • Respond — contain, investigate, and hand off.

The signals support sees first

SignalWhat it can mean
Failed logins spikePassword guessing or credential stuffing
Antivirus/EDR alertMalware or a false positive
Unexpected outbound trafficCommand-and-control or data exfiltration
New admin accountCompromise or an unmanaged change

Triage discipline

  1. Confirm the alert is real and not a known-good change.
  2. Assess impact: what data or systems are exposed?
  3. Preserve the evidence before touching anything.
  4. Escalate on policy, not on mood — when in doubt, report.

Measure the SOC on time-to-detect and time-to-respond, not on the number of alerts closed.
