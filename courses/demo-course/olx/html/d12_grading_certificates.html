Grading & Certificates

The demo course uses a realistic grading policy so the certificate leg can be tested end to end.

Grading policy

Assignment typeWeight
Homework40%
Final Exam60%

The pass threshold is 50%. A learner who answers the problem types correctly clears it comfortably.

What happens on a passing grade

  1. The LMS recomputes the course grade and crosses the pass threshold.
  2. Open edX's completion trigger enqueues certificate generation.
  3. A downloadable certificate is issued and appears on the learner's dashboard.
  4. The certificate bridge pushes the completion into Signara, which produces a signed, verifiable PDF with an audit trail.

Why this matters

A certificate that nobody can trust is worthless. AthenIQ issues Open edX certificates and has them signed by the platform's DocumentOps surface, so the credential a learner shares is verifiable.

Try it: complete the graded problem types and the final exam, then open your dashboard to see the certificate link.
