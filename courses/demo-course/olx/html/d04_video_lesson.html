Video & Audio

Open edX can host uploaded video through its video pipeline, or play external sources. This lesson embeds a public HTML5 sample directly so it renders on any deployment with no extra configuration.

Authoring notes

  • Uploaded video — use the platform's Video component and upload through Studio; the video pipeline transcodes and serves it.
  • External video — YouTube or an HTML5 source works without the pipeline, as shown above.
  • Transcripts and captions — always publish a transcript; it is required for accessibility and helps learners search the content.
  • Keep clips short — a few focused minutes beat a long recording.

Audio-only material works the same way: provide a player and a transcript.
