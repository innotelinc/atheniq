Where Pasted Text Goes

When you paste text into an AI tool, you are sending it to a service run by another organisation. What happens next depends on the contract, the settings, and the tool — not on what the chat window implies. This chapter is about deciding before you paste, because once text leaves, deleting the conversation does not bring it back.

Classify first

ClassExamplesRoute
PublicPublished reports, job postings, marketing copyAny approved tool
InternalMeeting notes with no personal data, internal proceduresApproved tool, per your policy
ConfidentialPricing, contracts, unreleased plans, source codeOnly the approved enterprise route with retention terms
RestrictedCustomer records, health or financial data, employee files, credentials, safeguarding notesNot pasted; use the system that owns the data

Two things people get wrong

"I deleted the chat." Deletion removes your copy from the interface. It does not undo a transfer, and it does not change whether the provider retained the content under its terms. Treat deletion as a tidy-up, not a control.

"It is just a summary." A summary still contains the data. Asking a tool to summarise a restricted document is the same transfer as pasting the document.

Personal accounts are the quiet risk

Most data incidents are not dramatic; they are a well-meaning employee using a personal account because the approved tool was slower, or because they did not know one existed. Two things prevent that: make the approved route easy, and make the rule unambiguous. "Ask if unsure" is not a rule — "restricted data never leaves the system that owns it" is.

What to do instead

  • Use the enterprise tool your organisation has reviewed, with the data turned off for training where the contract allows it.
  • Replace identifying detail with placeholders before drafting: "Customer A", "Site 2", "the 2024 contract".
  • Keep the source in your own system and ask the assistant to work from the structure, not the records.
  • If the task genuinely needs the restricted data, it needs the owning system, not a chat window.

Try this

Take eight real snippets from your week and classify each one. For every confidential or restricted item, write the approved route in one sentence. If you cannot write that sentence, that is the gap to raise with your manager — before the task, not after.
