Exam tips

  • Read the whole question before answering; many describe a scenario, not a fact.
  • When a question offers "investigate first" and "change something", investigation is usually right.
  • Prefer the answer that is reversible and documented.
  • For security questions, prefer isolate-and-report over clean-and-continue.

Where to go next

The IT Support Specialist track is the entry rung. From here you can specialise: deeper networking, systems administration, security operations, or cloud support. Stay curious, keep a lab you can break safely, and keep writing things down — the habit of documenting is the one that compounds.

Good luck with the final exam. Your certificate is issued automatically on a passing grade and signed through Signara.
