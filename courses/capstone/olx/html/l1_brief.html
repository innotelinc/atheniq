Capstone Brief & Portfolio

The capstone asks you to prove you can run support, not just answer questions about it. You will work through a simulated service desk and document your reasoning.

The brief

  1. Run the queue. A set of tickets arrives with different impacts and urgencies. Triage, prioritise, and resolve or escalate each one.
  2. Apply the method. Every resolution must follow the troubleshooting methodology from ITSP101: identify, theorise, test, plan, fix, verify, document.
  3. Escalate correctly. Decide what is beyond scope or access, and hand it off with complete notes.
  4. Write it up. Produce ticket notes a colleague could act on without asking you anything.

Your portfolio

Keep a short portfolio as you work — it is what you show an employer:

  • Two or three tickets you resolved, with the reasoning visible.
  • One problem you escalated, and why escalation was right.
  • One knowledge-base article you wrote from a repeat issue.
  • A one-paragraph reflection on what you would do differently.

What is assessed

AreaWhat good looks like
MethodEvidence-driven, one change at a time
PrioritisationImpact and urgency, not first-in-first-out
CommunicationPlain language, realistic expectations
DocumentationClear enough for the next technician

There is no trick here. A calm, methodical technician who documents well scores higher than a fast one who leaves mess behind.
