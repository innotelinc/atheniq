ITSP104 — IT Support Capstone

The final course of the Innotel Labs IT Support Specialist track. Prove you can run a service desk: triage a queue, apply the troubleshooting method, escalate correctly, and document work a colleague could act on.

What you will do

  • Work a simulated ticket queue end to end
  • Build a portfolio of resolved tickets and a knowledge-base article
  • Sit a certification review across the whole track
