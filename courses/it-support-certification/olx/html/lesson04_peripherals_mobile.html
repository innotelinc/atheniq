Peripherals & Mobile Devices

Most support requests are about what plugs into the computer — or about the computer in the user's pocket.

Common peripherals

  • Displays: a blank screen is a cabling, input, or cable-source problem until proven otherwise. Try another cable and another port before replacing the monitor.
  • Keyboards & mice: test wireless batteries and pairing first; a single dead key is usually a hardware fault, an entire unresponsive keyboard is usually a connection or driver fault.
  • Printers: check power, network or USB connection, paper, and the queue. Clear stuck jobs before reinstalling drivers.
  • Docking stations: often the cause of "my second monitor stopped working" — reseat the dock, update its firmware, and confirm the laptop's display settings.

Mobile devices

  • Battery: heat and battery drain are the top complaints. Check which apps consume power and whether the device is charging at all.
  • Connectivity: cellular data versus Wi-Fi; airplane mode is the quickest radio reset.
  • Storage: full storage causes failed updates and slow apps.
  • Updates: mobile OS updates carry security fixes and should not be deferred indefinitely.

Enrolling a mobile device

In a managed environment, phones and tablets are enrolled in MDM (mobile-device management) so policies, apps, and remote wipe are controlled centrally. Enrolment requires the user's informed consent — always explain what the organisation can and cannot see on the device.

Accessibility

Support is not only about replacing hardware. Screen readers, magnification, high-contrast themes, captions, and alternative input devices are standard accommodations. Know where the accessibility settings live on each platform.
