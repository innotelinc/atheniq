Welcome to ITSP101 — IT Support Foundations

This course prepares you for an entry-level IT support role and for a professional IT support certification. It is the first rung of the Innotel Labs IT Support Specialist track: seven chapters that build from "what is a computer" to running a real help desk.

What you will be able to do

  • Identify and service the core components of desktops, laptops, and mobile devices.
  • Install, configure, and troubleshoot Windows, macOS, and Linux.
  • Explain how networks move data and fix common connectivity failures.
  • Apply security fundamentals: malware response, phishing recognition, MFA.
  • Work a ticket queue: triage, document, escalate, and close.
  • Follow a repeatable troubleshooting methodology instead of guessing.

How the course is organized

Each chapter has two parts: a set of lessons to read, then a graded activity — a homework quiz or a hands-on lab. The labs ask you to reason through a realistic scenario exactly as you would at a service desk.

Grading

  • Homework (knowledge checks) — 35%
  • Labs (applied scenarios) — 25%
  • Final Exam — 40%

You need a total of 70% to pass. On a passing grade the platform automatically issues a course certificate; a signed, verifiable copy is produced through Signara.

Tip: keep a lab notebook. Write down every command, setting, and error message you meet. The habit of documenting your work is what separates a good support technician from a great one.
