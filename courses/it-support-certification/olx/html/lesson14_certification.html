Your Certification & Next Steps

You have covered the full breadth of entry-level IT support. Passing the final exam completes the Innotel Labs IT Support Foundations course and earns your certificate.

What you have learned

  • Hardware and devices: identifying and servicing components and peripherals.
  • Operating systems: Windows, macOS, and Linux administration and repair.
  • Networking: layers, IP, DNS, DHCP, and structured connectivity checks.
  • Security: threats, controls, phishing response, and incident escalation.
  • Service desk: ticketing, prioritization, SLAs, and communication.
  • Troubleshooting: a repeatable, evidence-driven method.

Continuing your track

This course is the foundation of the IT Support Specialist track. Natural next steps on the same path:

  • ITSP102 — Networking & Systems Support: deeper routing and switching, Windows Server and Linux administration.
  • ITSP103 — Security Operations Foundations: monitoring, incident response, and identity administration.
  • ITSP104 — Capstone: run a live service desk simulation and build your portfolio.

Preparing for the exam

  • Review each chapter's key terms and the "fast check" steps.
  • Practice explaining a fix in one minute, in plain language.
  • Re-read the labs: scenario reasoning is the most exam-relevant skill.

After you pass

Your certificate is issued automatically on a passing grade. A signed, verifiable copy is produced through Signara and linked from your learner dashboard — share it with employers as evidence of the skills above. Good luck.
