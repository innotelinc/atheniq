Accounts & Software

Most of a service desk's volume is identity and application work: access, installs, and sign-in problems.

Accounts and directory services

An account is an identity with rights. Directories such as Active Directory or Authentik store it and let single sign-on carry one login across many systems. Common tasks: create, disable, reset a password, and change group membership.

  • Least privilege: grant the minimum access needed for the job.
  • Group-based access: assign rights to groups, not individuals — it is easier to audit and to revoke.
  • Offboarding: disable accounts immediately when someone leaves, then remove access and recover devices.

Software installation and licensing

  • Install from a trusted, approved source. Untrusted installers are a leading malware vector.
  • Check the licence before installing — user, device, or subscription models differ.
  • Prefer central deployment (SCCM, Intune, package repos, MDM) over clicking through installers by hand.

Sign-in troubleshooting, in order

  1. Confirm the username and that Caps Lock is off.
  2. Reset the password through the approved self-service flow.
  3. Check whether the account is locked out or disabled.
  4. Verify multi-factor enrolment and clock/time sync on the device.
  5. Clear cached credentials or re-authenticate the app; only then involve the identity team.

Backups

Before any risky change, confirm the user's data is backed up and know how to restore it. A support technician who can recover data is never the cause of a catastrophe.
