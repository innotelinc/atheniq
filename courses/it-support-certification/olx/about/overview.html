ITSP101 — IT Support Foundations

The first course of the Innotel Labs IT Support Specialist certification track. Learn to support users, devices, operating systems, networks, and security — then practise it on a realistic help desk.

Seven chapters take you from hardware fundamentals to a repeatable troubleshooting method. Graded homework and applied labs build toward a final exam; a passing grade issues a signed, verifiable certificate through Signara.

What you will learn

  • Hardware and device support
  • Windows, macOS, and Linux administration
  • Networking: IP, DNS, DHCP, and connectivity checks
  • Security fundamentals and incident response
  • Service desk operations, ticketing, and SLAs
  • A structured troubleshooting methodology
